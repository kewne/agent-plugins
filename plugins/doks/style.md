# Writing Style Guide

All documentation files must follow a clear, consistent writing style.

## Guidelines

1. **Short and to the Point**: Keep sentences and phrases short, direct, and focused on a single idea. Omit filler words and unnecessary preamble.
2. **Plain Language**: Prefer simple, everyday words over complex vocabulary or jargon. Write in active voice whenever possible.
3. **Strict Glossary Usage**: Always use the exact term defined in its `<term>.glossary.md` file. Never substitute synonyms, abbreviations, or informal aliases for defined glossary terms.
4. **Cross-Reference Once Per Section**: Link each cross-referenced term or document only on its first mention within a section. Leave subsequent mentions in the same section unlinked to avoid cluttering the text.
